# Northwind Home Services: who we are and what we sell

## Who we are

Northwind Home Services is a cleaning and handyman company at 412 Forest Avenue, Portland, Maine, owned and run by Dana Whitfield. We started in residential cleaning and handyman work. Light-commercial cleaning (condo associations, offices, medical and dental practices, restaurants and rental property managers) is now most of the roughly $2.8 million we invoice a year. About 22 people, 3 cleaning crews, 1 handyman crew and 4 vans. Jordan Lee runs sales, Marcus Reyes runs operations, Elena Marsh runs finance.

## What we sell and how it is priced

Commercial work is priced per building per month, flat, after a walk-through.

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Apartment building common areas, 1 visit a week (entries, halls, stairwells, laundry room, trash room) | $120 to $260 per building per month, by units and floors |
| Portfolio of 25 or more buildings on one route | up to 10% off the per-building price |
| Condo common areas or office cleaning, 3 to 5 days a week | $5,000 to $19,000 per building per month at our current accounts |
| Office, clinic or restaurant, 1 visit a week | $1,800 to $3,900 per month |
| Small office or practice, 2 evening visits a month | $480 to $1,150 per month, flat |
| Rental turnovers | $275 to $330 per unit |
| Handyman | $385 to $640 per job. From October 1, 2026, any job over 2 hours gets a site visit before we quote |

Every commercial price includes a photo log after each visit and a monthly service report.

## Where we are today

- 18 commercial accounts on a recurring schedule, at 35 sites, plus turnovers for 3 rental property managers.
- Our largest account is Eastern Promenade Condominium Association: 11 buildings, 340 units, common areas 5 days a week, held for two and a half years.
- **Capacity.** Current crews can absorb up to 8 small buildings a quarter on a weekly visit without hiring. Anything bigger needs a new crew: 4 cleaners and a van, which covers about 60 small apartment buildings a week. Hiring 4 takes about 3 weeks, and we add at most 1 new crew a quarter.

## The arrival-window guarantee

Every commercial visit has a written arrival window. If the crew arrives after the window closes, the customer gets a credit of **$400 per missed window**, as a line on the next monthly statement. Residential customers get $45 per scheduled labor hour. Every miss appears in the monthly report, with the cause and the fix.

## What we don't do

- Snow and grounds: we subcontract to Greenline Lawn & Snow and manage them for the customer.
- Plumbing and other licensed trades: we subcontract (Tidewater Plumbing for plumbing).
- Pest control: no.
- Exterior windows above the second floor: no. We refer a specialty vendor with a lift.

## How Dana sells

Start small and earn the rest. A new multi-building customer gets a 60-day pilot on one building at the full rate. If the pilot meets the arrival windows and the report is useful, we expand building by building. Lead with the report and the references, not the price.

## Why customers pick us

- We tell you when we miss. Written arrival windows, a credit when we are late, and the miss is in the report.
- One report an owner or a board can read in 5 minutes: arrival times, missed windows, issues found, photos.
- The same crew lead at your building every week.
- Cleaning and small repairs from one company. The crew that finds the broken door closer reports it, and our handyman crew fixes it.
